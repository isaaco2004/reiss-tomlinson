About

He / Him

© Tom Barker

Actor based in Greater Manchester, recently seen as Young Danny Sloane in the BBC's Ludwig.

Reiss played the lead in the short film Condensation, which premiered at the SCENE Film Festival at HOME. He co-founded the theatre company Theatre On Draught, which debuted its first play, The Divine Move, at the Greater Manchester Fringe and continues to make new work.

He trained in acting at the University of Lancashire and completed the National Youth Theatre's summer course this year. Away from acting, Reiss is writing and developing original music.
